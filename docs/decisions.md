# Latest approved RAYAX decisions

The current product page is the visual baseline. Do not reconstruct already approved parts from conversation history.

- Persian RTL, the nine supplied Estedad FD v7.3 TTF weights, official Phosphor icons and the supplied SVG logo. All assets are in `src/assets`.
- Minimum text size 12px. The earlier one-pass migration was 10→12 and 12→13; do not repeatedly increase already migrated sizes.
- Brand blue #2546F5, light surfaces #F1F5F8 and white, text #2E3344. Added navy accents and dark borders in product content were removed; the reference footer remains dark.
- Desktop header is sticky; the separate Snapp Pay banner scrolls away. Header and main content have the same max width, 1280px, on all pages. Menu weights are 600/700.
- Cart and profile panels open under the main header row, aligned to their own triggers. Mega menu opens under the navigation row.
- Adding a product directly opens the cart. Quantity controls are inside it; sample cap is five per configuration. All key-feature values are right-aligned, including LTR processor names.
- Old struck-through price is above the new price. Comparison is removed. Input focus uses a brand border without outline/offset.
- Product cards use the latest supplied reference: brand upper-left, centered picture, two-line title, category badge and blue price; the whole card opens details.
- Introduction is an authored article with banners, a local 12-second image-montage video and an expandable continuation. The quick-stat strip and visible Apple-site link are removed.
- Reviews have a summary sidebar, score distribution, helpful votes and more-results controls; buyer filter, sorting, sample labels and report controls are removed. Display scores with one yellow star and X/5. The star-rating form requires prototype sign-in.
- Login, prices, inventory, policies and sample reviews are demonstrative; there is no payment or authentication backend. Keep prototype disclosures in sign-in and checkout dialogs; remove helper/demo copy from the product page.
- Mobile: sticky two-row header (menu/logo/account/cart, then full-width search) with Snapp Pay below it. All dialogs are bottom sheets except the fullscreen gallery. The fixed purchase bar switches to configuration-aware quantity controls. Benefits, footer services and related products scroll horizontally without visible scrollbars; product tabs are edge-to-edge. Gallery has a light background and hides the image counter on mobile. One skewed footer rectangle spans its height.

- Product article paragraphs are 14px and justified, using the full article width. FAQ questions are 16px/600 and answers 14px/500. Technical values sit close to their keys and use weight 600. Purchase button is compact, with tighter old/new price spacing. Gallery surfaces are transparent.
- Home page uses the approved shared header/footer, dialogs, product-card styles and mobile navigation. Home-only sections and interactions are in home.html, home.css and 11-home.js. Both standalone and folder previews link home and product pages together.

- Catalog page follows PLP.png: right-side desktop filters, four-column results, brand strip, sorting, load more and buying guide. Mobile uses two columns and a focus-managed filter sheet. The product-card HTML renderer is shared by home, related products and catalog in scripts/12-product-card.js; shared card styles are in shared/catalog-cards.css. Catalog inventory, prices and ranking are local prototype data.

- Catalog review labels show stable section IDs at the upper left. When the user references an ID, inspect and edit only that section and directly required shared dependencies.

- Checkout lives in one page, `cart` (`pages/cart.html`, `scripts/14-checkout.js`, `shared/checkout.css`), styled after the supplied Purchase Flow reference in RAYAX colors: `#cart → #details → #payment → #success|#failed`. No wallet. Leaving the cart requires sign-in in a modal (mobile, then five separate OTP boxes with “edit number” under the text). The details step has three separate panels: buyer info (name, surname, national ID with checksum, birth date, verified mobile, optional email), delivery address, and shipping method and delivery day only (no time slots); fields carry Phosphor icons and focus uses a soft blue border. All data is local prototype data. Signed-in account menu: user card plus orders, addresses, favorites and logout.
- Cart page content is 1100px wide on a #fafafa canvas with white 16px-radius panels; main/summary columns use a 1.618:1 ratio. Blue only on primary actions, soft-blue for "add address"; selections are low-contrast gray; fields use the project focus (1px brand border, no ring); completed steps are green. Success/failure pages are receipts with a faint logo watermark.
- Product buy card shows a Snapp Pay 4-installment row under the add-to-cart button (monthly amount follows the selected configuration); it opens an installment schedule dialog. Figures are demonstrative.
- Checkout selections use a dark ticked radio and a dark 1px card border; delivery days are a 4-column grid (no scroll); the summary lists items openly and clamps after five lines with a fade and show more/less.
- Cart page hides the shared header/footer (kept in DOM for shared scripts) and uses its own minimal header (logo, secure-checkout badge, back to shop) and a black #111 footer (support, trust points, copyright). Mobile stepper is a three-segment progress track with labels.
- RAYAX Mag has a magazine index and a single-article template with shared editorial content in scripts/16-magazine-data.js. Magazine pages use navy, blue, white and gray, fine patterns, and light-gray image placeholders; no sample photos. Index supports topics, search, sorting and more results. Articles support an anchored contents list, reading progress, local saved state and copying the link. The existing shared header/footer are preserved.
- 2026-10-19: Product reviews omit recommendation and helpful controls, use circular user icons and scroll horizontally on mobile. Gallery thumbnails sit at the bottom; its title remains inside the dialog. Checkout header has back/title on the right and logo on the left, a joined mobile progress track, green continuation buttons, centered mobile fields and compact verification icons. Visible prototype/helper copy and PLP reference labels are removed at the user's request; IDs remain available in source. This remains a local prototype without live authentication/payment.
